## Decisions Made

- Single-source evidence assembly lives in query-server.ts (assembleArrangementReviewEvidence); the peer path consumes it through boot's injected dependency. The CLI review path intentionally runs the pure analyzers twice (prepareArrangeDispatch preamble + builder) — deterministic and documented as the price of one extraction serving both surfaces
- Per-scene sparkline energy uses SectionSummary.energy (the per-scene aggregate the analyzers emit); the per-bar energyCurve is surfaced as bounded stats (bars/peak) rather than mis-mapped per-bar chars
- resolveAnalysisContext enrichment is additive-spread (`...(await loadArrangementAnalysisEvidence(...))`) — it can never break the existing project/selectedClip context shape and never throws

## Phase Goal

**As a** producer, **I want to** preview a patch as a diff, apply it, and revert it through daemon-authoritative history, **so that** no mutation can wreck my song without a reversible, labelled, risk-classified patch object.

## Plan Objective (vertical slice 2: trust-spine end-to-end I/O)

Plan 01 shipped the proven pure primitives. This plan wires them into the **live apply/revert pipeline**: ephemeral candidate store (D-05) → durable patch-history.jsonl journal (D-03) → `bw-edit preview/apply/revert` CLI (D-04) → daemon UDS dispatch → bridge `case "apply.patch"` NoteStep mutation. After this plan a producer can `bw-edit preview`, `bw-edit apply --confirm`, and `bw-edit revert` end-to-end, with every apply journalled with inverse ops (daemon-authoritative undo, EDIT-05).

**Purpose:** EDIT-02 (CLI preview), EDIT-04 (apply), EDIT-05 (history + revert), EDIT-06 (apply-time risk gate). The wire contract is proven by a daemon↔fake-bridge smoke test (no live Bitwig needed).
**Output:** candidate-store.ts, patch-history.ts, edit.ts (live), query-server edit.* dispatch, bridge handleApplyPatch, smoke test.

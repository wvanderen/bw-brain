## Phase Goal

**As a** producer, **I want to** preview a patch as a diff, apply it, and revert it through daemon-authoritative history, **so that** no mutation can wreck my song without a reversible, labelled, risk-classified patch object.

## Plan Objective (vertical slice 1: trust-spine foundation, proven by property tests)

This plan ships the **pure trust-spine primitives + their JSON-Schema contract + the fast-check harness**, all property-tested in isolation. After this plan: a Patch validates against a frozen schema, resolves against Note[] state, self-inverts, round-trips, and is risk-classified — all proven by fast-check on arbitrary op sequences. This is the foundation Plans 02–05 build the I/O, bridge, transforms, and UX on top of.

**Purpose:** EDIT-01 (schema), EDIT-02/03 (pure preview), EDIT-05 (pure inverse), EDIT-06 (pure risk) — the heart of the trust spine. Validation IS the deliverable (INV-1,2,3,4,5,9,10).
**Output:** `schemas/patch.schema.json` + `schemas/profile.schema.json` + 4 tightened/extended schemas + `daemon/src/patch/{patch-schema,inverse-ops,patch-resolve,risk-classifier,arb}.ts` + their fast-check tests + deps (fast-check, tonal).

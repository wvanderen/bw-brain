## Required Invariants

- **RT-1:** Audio callback performs no allocation, blocking, I/O, JSON, logging, or UI work.
- **RT-2:** Original audio and input MIDI remain transparent in every idle/failure/generated state.
- **RT-3:** Queues, aggregates, phrases, and work per block are strictly bounded; only observational telemetry may coalesce/drop.
- **MIDI-1:** Every generated note-on receives exactly one owned note-off across completion and all lifecycle interruptions.
- **ROUTE-1:** Controller and CLAP traffic are isolated; every CLAP send names one authorized peer.
- **AUTH-1:** Unconfirmed/stale instances cannot prompt, approve, arm, or mutate; stale/consumed grants fail closed.
- **JOURNAL-1:** Existing-state mutation uses the current controller trust spine and preserves history/inverse semantics.
- **SESSION-1:** Reopen resumes, duplication rekeys, Save As explicitly forks, and histories diverge.

## Native Scheduler Matrix

Cover float/double, mono/stereo, in-place/out-of-place, zero/non-zero offsets, same-sample ordering, notes spanning blocks, beat/bar boundaries, tempo and time-signature changes, absent transport flags, loop/jump/stop, output push failure, disconnect, reset, deactivate, maximum bounds, and limit+1 rejection.

## Decisions Made

- A repeated byte-equivalent proposal returns its current revision; only a content or target change creates a new immutable revision and invalidates outstanding grants.
- Failed scope revalidation also retires the presented token, preventing a drifted or malicious attempt from becoming valid later.
- The dispatcher synthesizes only the `RawState.selection.clipSid` view required by `EditService.apply` from the immutable confirmed proposal scope, avoiding current-focus races without weakening candidate pre-flight.

## Decisions Made

- **Found during:** Overall RT/transparency scan
- **Issue:** Rebuilding every original event as three MIDI bytes would truncate sysex and non-MIDI payloads and used callback-local replacement storage.
- **Fix:** Leave original host events untouched and append only bounded generated events in sample order.
- **Files modified:** `clap/src/PluginProcessor.cpp`
- **Verification:** Full native product suite passes; structural scan finds no callback-local replacement allocation.

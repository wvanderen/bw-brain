**A dedicated connection-aware CLAP endpoint now validates and bounds every peer independently while targeted sends remain mechanically isolated from sibling peers and controller TCP.**

## Accomplishments

- Added literal-loopback peer listening with port 7879 default, per-socket connection IDs, split-line framing, compile-once schema validation, and handshake timeouts.
- Added an accepted-peer registry whose only outbound primitive is `sendTo(connectionId, envelope)`; invalid targets or envelopes refuse without broadcast.
- Proved phrase delivery reaches CLAP A only while CLAP B and a controller connection receive zero bytes, plus deterministic oversize, invalid, queue overflow, timeout, and disconnect behavior.
- Wired peer startup and shutdown beside the existing controller TCP and CLI UDS lifecycles without changing their implementation or mutation authority.
